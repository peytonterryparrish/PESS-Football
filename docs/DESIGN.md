# Proper Football: design

Source of truth for decisions. Update it when a decision changes.

## Pillars
1. **Simple everywhere.** Controller in hand to kick-off in under a minute. Few screens, A selects, B backs out.
2. **Controller-first.** Every screen is driven by stick + A/B (+X/Y/LB as shortcuts). Keyboard is a fallback.
3. **Offline only.** Local save files (`Saved/SaveGames/career.pfsave`). No accounts, servers or online subsystem.
4. **Play or manage every match.** Play it with the pad, manage it (fast "dots" sim with slowed-down sprite highlights), or take an instant result.
5. **Talent you can see.** Stats map onto extreme, non-linear differences on the pitch (below).

## Camera and look (studied from SNES-era footage)
- The owner's stadiums are finished 4K atlases with a diagonal camera and an affine projection (`Projection.json`): world metres (x along the pitch, y across, z up) map straight to atlas pixels. The match sim runs in metres; everything is projected onto the atlas.
- Reference measurements from SNES footage: players fill about 14% of the screen's height; the view shows about 30 m of pitch; goal lines run diagonally; the controlled player gets a coloured ring; there's a minimap at bottom centre, names at the bottom corners, score and clock at the top.
- Our camera is pulled out a touch: about 40 m across. At 1080p each stadium pixel is 6 screen pixels and each sprite pixel is 2. The owner chose to keep the stadium/sprite pixel-size difference.
- Everything is drawn on the HUD canvas (no 3D scene): exact colours, no tone mapping, no AA, no bloom or motion blur, nearest sampling everywhere, positions rounded to whole screen pixels.

## Sprites: PixelLab static poses, animation built in code
- **Base characters.** 16 outfield bodies and 4 keepers come from the owner's south-facing idles (chosen for skin/hair/build variety), plus a referee, a linesman and 3 managers from the match-engine pack.
- **Template kit.** Outfield bodies are recoloured into a template kit (blue shirt, green shorts, red socks, magenta trim) before generation (`Tools/pipeline/kit.py`). After generation every kit pixel is snapped to a key colour (region code + shade), and the sprite material recolours it into any club's kit at run time, including stripes, hoops and halves. One pose set serves all 116 clubs.
- **Rotations.** PixelLab `generate-8-rotations-v3` turns each idle into 8 static directions.
- **Poses.** Each pose is a still, generated by `animate-with-skeleton-v3` from a skeleton that **we** define in code: a 3D stick figure posed with joint angles (`Tools/pixellab/poses.py`, `poses_more.py`), projected into each direction. PixelLab's text-to-animation is never used. Five directions are generated (S, SE, E, NE, N); SW, W and NW are mirrored in code.
- **Pose library.** Outfield: run cycle (6), sprint (4), jog (2), walk (2), idle breathe, dribble touches (2), turn, shot (4), pass (2), volley, chest, header (stand, jump, dive), slide, standing tackle, block, shield, throw-in (2), stumble, fall, sit, get-up, celebrations (5), hands on head. Keepers: ready, shuffle (2), low/high dives left/right (2 stages each), catches, punch, lying with ball, throw (2), punt (2), hold. Officials: walk/jog/run, whistle, point, card, advantage, flag up, flag out. Managers: idle, walk, shout, arms folded, frustrated, point, celebrate.
- **QA** (`Tools/pipeline/build_atlas.py`): hard alpha, stray specks removed, baked-in balls removed, doubled figures rejected, size check against the idle, edge-touch and colour-drift flags, palette clamp to the character's own palette. Rejected frames fall back to the neighbouring direction or the idle at run time. Contact sheets: `ArtWork/sheets/`. Reports: `ArtWork/qa/`.
- **Animation in code** (`PFSprites.cpp`, `PFMatch.cpp`): locomotion frames advance by **distance travelled**, so feet stay planted and fast players cycle faster. Actions are timed clips scaled by each player's animation rate (pace/agility); the ball leaves on the clip's contact frame.

## Talent you can see (stat -> pitch)
In `PFAbility()`; stats are 1..99.

| Stat | Effect | Poor (20) -> great (90) |
|---|---|---|
| Pace | top speed | 3.9 -> 8.6 m/s (curve ^1.35) |
| Acceleration | m/s^2 | ~5 -> ~24 |
| Agility | turn rate | ~230 -> ~880 deg/s |
| Ball control | dribble touch distance; first-touch miscontrols | 2.4 m heavy touches -> 0.7 m glued |
| Passing / shooting | aim error, power | 12 deg -> 2 deg; shot 20 -> 31 m/s |
| Reactions | AI think/tackle delay, keeper reaction | 0.55 s -> 0.1 s |
| Pace + agility | animation playback rate | 0.8x -> 1.25x |

## Career
- **Pyramid.** Premier Division (20), The Championship (24), League One (24), League Two (24), National League (24). The 93 clubs come from the owner's packs, placed by their 2005-06 division; the heritage club and 23 generated non-league sides make up the fifth tier. Movement between tiers: 3, 3, 4 and 2 places.
- **Cups.** The Association Cup (all 116 clubs) and The League Trophy (top four tiers). Single-match knockout ties; shoot-outs are simulated.
- **Season.** 46 weeks. Every club plays its league round when it falls on that week; cup rounds fall on fixed weeks. Other matches use a strength-based Poisson model.
- **Manager.** Starts at a random League Two club. Board confidence moves after every match against an expected-points curve; under 10 after week 15 means the sack. **Relegation always means the sack.** A season finish well below expectation (confidence under 20) also means the sack. Reputation rises and falls with results against expectation, promotions and trophies, and sets which clubs offer jobs each summer. Out of work, you pick from clubs a level or so below your standing.
- **Transfers.** Windows in weeks 1-4 and 22-25. Values grow exponentially with overall rating (scaled for age, potential and contract); wage demands work the same way. A user bid is accepted if the fee meets the club's price (more for a starter, less if he's listed), and the player then picks the best offer, weighted by wage and club stature. AI clubs bid against you, buy from lower tiers, and make offers for players you list.
- **Youth.** Every club takes in an academy intake each season. Each world region has a youth pool. Scouts (up to 4, quality 1-4) go to a region for four weeks and bring back names. Better scouts find higher-potential kids. **Stats are never shown for prospects.** You can watch a youth match in any region; every kid in it joins your list, and the talented ones are visibly faster and cleaner. Sign them to the academy, promote them later.
- **People.** Development is age-based toward a hidden potential. Over-30s decline, and pace goes first. Retirements start at 34; regens arrive every season; unsigned 19-year-olds drift out of the game.

## UI
- All screens are drawn on the HUD canvas with two pixel fonts built from the owner's UI glyph sprites, the owner's badges, tactics shirts and 4K broadcast backgrounds. One list/menu system covers every screen (stick + A/B), which keeps everything pad-navigable. UMG/CommonUI was considered and skipped: the canvas menus are simpler to keep pixel-crisp and controller-only.

## Tooling
- `Tools/pipeline/build_data.py`: clubs, fonts, badges, shirts, ball and UI sprites into `GameData/`.
- `Tools/pixellab/produce.py`: resumable, parallel, budget-capped production (`--floor` = minimum account balance).
- `Tools/pipeline/build_atlas.py`: QA + atlases + contact sheets.
- `Tools/ue/make_assets.py`: the only Unreal assets (sprite material, empty map).
- Test hooks: `-pfmatch`, `-pfwatch`, `-pfshots=N -pfshotgap=S`, `-pfcareertest=N`, `-pfcareerscreen=N`.
