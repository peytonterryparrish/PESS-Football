# England-only map provenance

The land silhouette was rasterized from the Office for National Statistics **Countries (December 2024) Boundaries UK BUC**, selecting only England (ONS code E92000001). The BUC product is clipped to the coastline and ultra-generalised at 500 m. Other UK nations are intentionally not rendered. The same official polygons drive both views.

- Dataset: https://geoportal.statistics.gov.uk/datasets/ons::countries-december-2024-boundaries-uk-buc-2/about
- ONS FeatureServer: https://services1.arcgis.com/ESMARspQHYMw9BZ9/arcgis/rest/services/Countries_December_2024_Boundaries_UK_BUC/FeatureServer
