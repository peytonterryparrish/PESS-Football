UNTITLED RETRO FOOTBALL UI / 93-CLUB TACTICS JERSEYS
==================================================
The original 93-club R08 south-idle roster is the artwork source.
The original R08 garment pixels have been remapped onto transparent,
front-facing tactics shirts. Club colors, striping, shoulder panels
and pixel dither come from the R08 designs. These are not a new set of
historically authenticated kits or replicas of today's shirts.

372 shirt sprites at 72×64 (four per club: home, away, goalkeeper 1 and 2).
Three 3840×2160 jersey sheets plus JSON and CSV mapping by club slug.
The existing 1,795 modular UI assets and their nine 4K sheets are included.
Arsenal squad and Newcastle tactics screens use their roster-derived shirts.
Shirt numbers and position labels shown in the mockups are separate overlays;
no numbers are baked into the shirt PNGs. Use point/nearest filtering.
Metadata lists the source sprite path, palette references, and atlas rect.
The home, away and goalkeeper player sprites themselves were not changed.

Still includes Youth Academy. No game title, collectible player cards or packs.
The examples are visuals only; no game project was edited.
